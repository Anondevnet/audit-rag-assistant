Method notes

  • Recall@k (all gold): Fraction of answerable questions where every gold chunk_id appears in the top k retrieved chunks.
  • Mean recall@k: Macro-average of |gold ∩ retrieved@k| / |gold| on answerable questions.
  • MRR: Mean reciprocal rank of the first gold chunk in the retrieved list, on answerable questions.
  • Citation accuracy: On non-refusal answers, each claim sentence must have a [rank:chunk_id] marker, the chunk must be in the retrieved set, and at least half of the claim's content tokens (min 3, or all if fewer) must appear in the cited chunk. Refusals with no citations score 1.0.
  • Faithfulness: Same lexical support test as citation accuracy. This is not an LLM-as-judge score and can miss valid paraphrases.
  • Refusal accuracy: (true refusals + true answers) / N, using must_refuse from the golden set.
  • Refusal rule: The extractive generator also refuses when a proper-noun or identifier token from the question is missing from the retrieved chunks, or when a question asks for an exact dollar/amount loss and the candidate answer has no currency figure. This is lexical, not an LLM judge.

Sources: SWC Registry (MIT) and not-so-smart-contracts (Apache-2.0). This is a demo, not an audit.
